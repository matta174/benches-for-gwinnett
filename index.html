    Who has to permit a bench
    Gwinnett County DOT557 stops

    County maintained right of way. Two thirds of the system, and 506 of these stops already have a sidewalk. One conversation covers most of the network.

    • ROW permits page
    • DOTRowPermits@gwinnettcounty.com · 770.822.7440
    • Utility Accommodation in ROW ordinance (PDF)
    • 75 Langley Drive, Lawrenceville GA 30046

    Transit side, for stop locations and ridership: Transit@GwinnettCounty.com · 770.822.5010

    Georgia DOT259 stops

    State routes. Buford Hwy (SR 13), Lawrenceville Hwy (US 29), Beaver Ruin (SR 378), Jimmy Carter (SR 140), Stone Mountain Hwy (US 78). Highest ridership, hardest permit. Gwinnett sits in GDOT District 1, Area 1.

    • Permitting and GPAS portal
    • Driveway and Encroachment Control, Ch. 7 (PDF)
    • Area office contacts (PDF) · D1 Area 1: 770.535.5759

    Read this before you trust it. Sidewalk means a sidewalk polygon exists near the stop. It says nothing about width, condition, or whether there is room for a bench without blocking the pedestrian route. GTFS coordinates can sit 30 to 60 ft off the actual pole. Trips per weekday is service, not ridership: it tells you how long you wait, not how many people wait. Every stop needs eyes on it before anything gets built.
